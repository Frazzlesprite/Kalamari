About Kalamari

A small studio with a long view

Kalamari is an independent software studio based in Canberra, Australia. We design and develop productivity and wellbeing apps across finance and accounting, wellbeing, productivity, management, and pet care.

We give every product the time it needs to earn its place on someone's home screen. No growth hacks, no noise, just useful software that respects the people using it.

01

Privacy first

Our apps collect as little data as possible. No advertising, no ad tracking, and no selling your information.

02

Calm by design

Clear interfaces, sensible defaults, and no dark patterns. Software should reduce stress, not add to it.

03

Made in Canberra

Independent and locally owned. Support and decisions come from people close to the products and close to home.

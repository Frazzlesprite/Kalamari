## Product Overview

**One-liner:** Software that helps you work well and live well.

**What it does:** Kalamari designs and builds productivity and wellbeing apps in
Canberra, Australia. It ships them under its own name, sells them once rather
than by subscription, and keeps them small enough to maintain without a team.

**Product category:** independent software studio. The site is not a services
brochure; it is the credibility page for the apps and a place to send feedback.

**Product type:** studio with first-party products, not an agency and not SaaS.

**Business model:** revenue comes from one-time app purchases (PuppyPulse,
Whysper, Sitefile). No subscriptions, no advertising, no data sales. There is no
consulting offer on the site today.

**Current products and state:** PuppyPulse (Android, launching on Play),
Whysper (Android, built, not yet listed), Sitefile (Android in build; not yet on
the site). `[needs the owner]` Is the studio taking client work at all, or is the
site purely a shopfront for the apps?

## Target Audience

**Target visitors:** two kinds, in this order.
1. Someone deciding whether to install a Kalamari app. They want the product,
   the Play link for anything live, and enough proof that the studio is real.
2. Someone who wants something made. They want to see the standard of the work
   and a way to reach the person who does it.

**Owner-stated, 2026-11-18:** the site is a platform to inform and direct people
to the Play Store links for live products, and a showcase for people who might
want to reach out to have something made. Both jobs are real; the products come
first on the page.

**Decision-maker:** the individual. There is no procurement.

**Primary use case:** install an app, or start a conversation about building
one.

**Jobs to be done:**
- When I am about to install one of these apps, I want to know who made it, so I
  can judge whether it will still exist next year.
- When I have a problem or an idea, I want one obvious way to reach the person
  who built it.

**Use cases:** app discovery from a store listing, deciding whether to install,
and starting a build enquiry.

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** small independent studios are invisible next to the app
factories, and the store listing alone gives a buyer no way to check who is
behind an app.

**Why alternatives fall short:** agency sites sell services the visitor does not
want; studio "about" pages talk about process instead of stance.

**What it costs them:** uncertainty at the moment of purchase, which shows up as
a lower install rate rather than a complaint.

**Emotional tension:** "is this a real business, or a weekend project that will
stop updating in March?"

## Competitive Landscape

**Direct:** other small Australian app studios, most of which sell client work.

**Secondary:** the app factories and template shops whose listings crowd the

**Indirect:** a buyer's default assumption that any app might be abandoned.

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators:**
- One-time pricing across the catalogue.
- Privacy first: no ad tracking, no data sale, as little collection as possible.
- Calm by design: no dark patterns, no growth hacks, no notification pressure.
- Made in Canberra: independent, locally owned, support from the person who
  built it.

**How we do it differently:** the stance is stated as four principles on the
site and enforced in the apps (no ad SDK, no subscription, no account for
Whysper or Sitefile).

**Why that's better:** it answers "will this still be here, and will it behave"
without a team page or a funding announcement.

**Why customers choose it:** they do not choose a studio; they choose an app and
then check the studio. The studio's job is to survive that check.

## Brand Voice

| Attribute | Means | Doesn't mean | Example line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Plain | Short sentences, concrete nouns | Blunt or cold | "No growth hacks, no noise, just useful software." |
| Settled | States a position and moves on | Arrogant | "We give every product the time it needs to earn its place." |
| Local | Canberra named without flags or gimmicks | Parochial | "Independent and locally owned." |
| Quiet | No exclamation marks, no urgency | Timid | "Software should reduce stress, not add to it." |

**Tone by context:**
- Support: direct, first person, no ticket-speak.
- Launch: the product's voice, not the studio's.
- Errors: what happened, what to do, no blame.
- Sales: none. There is no sales page and no pitch.
- Social: the same sentences as the site.

**Terms we own:** "calm by design", "privacy first", "one payment".

**Never use (and why):** "solutions" and "empowering" (empty), "AI-powered"
(there is no AI in any product), and any comparative claim about competitors
that cannot be sourced.

## Goals

**Business goal:** two jobs, in order (owner-stated, 2026-11-18). Primary:
direct people to the Play listing of every live product. Secondary: showcase the
work so someone with an idea can start a conversation. A product with no live
listing stays "coming soon" and does not get a store link.

**Key conversion action:** a click to a Play listing for a live app; then the
contact form for build enquiries. That form has no endpoint configured
(`FORM_ENDPOINT` is empty in `assets/js/main.js`), so it currently tells the
visitor, honestly, that nothing was sent. Wiring it is a prerequisite for the
showcase job.
